Product price history tracker & browsing insights for shopping sites

Tracks product prices you view on supported stores and shows the price trend so you can decide when to buy.

Tags: price history · price tracker · price trend · price drop · aliexpress price · browsing history

Overview

Deciding whether a current price is a good deal is hard without knowing the history. This script records the product prices you view on supported store pages such as AliExpress, eBay, Amazon and Lazada, and shows the price trend for those products. Open the panel from the floating button to review your browsing history and the recorded price points for each product. Price history is built from pages you actually visit and is stored locally, so it only covers products you have viewed.

How to install & use

  1. Install a userscript manager such as Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey for your browser.
  2. Click the "Download / install" button and confirm in the manager.
  3. Open the target site and use the script as documented below.

Configuration

Open the panel from the floating button to see the products you have browsed and their recorded price points. Choose how long the local history is kept and whether price changes trigger a small badge. Language follows the browser and can be switched manually.

1. Browse products on a supported store such as AliExpress or eBay as you normally would. 2. Open the panel to see each product with its recorded price history. 3. Compare the current price with past prices and decide the right time to buy.

Privacy & permissions

The script reads the product page to record the product name and price, and stores this browsing history only in your browser's local storage. This feature does not upload history and does not send requests to third-party services.
