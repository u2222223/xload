YouTube SponsorBlock Controls

Control SponsorBlock skipping and replay skipped segments on YouTube.

Tags: YouTube · SponsorBlock · Playback

Overview

Open the floating button to inspect SponsorBlock segments in a separate panel. Choose automatic, manual, or disabled skipping and select segment categories. Replay a skipped segment without immediately skipping it again.

How to install & use

  1. Install a userscript manager such as Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey for your browser.
  2. Click the "Download / install" button and confirm in the manager.
  3. Open the target site and use the script as documented below.

Configuration

Open the panel to choose skipping mode and categories. Manual mode is the initial setting; mode, categories, and language preferences are remembered.

Open a YouTube video, open the panel, inspect returned segments, and skip one manually or enable automatic skipping. Use Replay to watch the last skipped segment.

Privacy & permissions

The script reads the current video identifier and playback position and sends the identifier to SponsorBlock to retrieve segments. Mode and category preferences stay in userscript storage; the panel language preference, diagnostic logs, and FAB position use local browser storage. Segment cache and replay state stay in session memory. The xload.net panel receives video and segment state through messaging and loads its page and assets. YouTube, SponsorBlock, and xload.net receive normal connection data for their respective requests.
