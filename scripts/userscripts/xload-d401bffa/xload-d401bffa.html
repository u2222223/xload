Discord bulk delete messages with preview and stop controls

Preview and delete messages in Discord channels or DMs with adjustable delays, progress counts, and a stop control.

Tags: Discord · bulk deletion · direct messages

Overview

Clean up message history in Discord channels or delete Discord DMs from the browser. Preview the selected scope, confirm deletion, and follow progress with adjustable delays and a stop control. Open the independent panel from the floating button or userscript menu. A signed-in account with the required permissions is necessary, deletion cannot be undone, and account automation may risk account termination. Searches and deletions go to Discord, while the panel receives scope details and progress without message bodies or credentials.

How to install & use

  1. Install a userscript manager such as Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey for your browser.
  2. Click the "Download / install" button and confirm in the manager.
  3. Open the target site and use the script as documented below.

Configuration

Open the panel from the floating button or userscript menu; select a channel, DM, or explicit server scope and author, then adjust the deletion delay from the default 1500 ms within 1000–60000 ms. Preview is required before deletion; language and delay preferences are remembered locally.

Sign in to Discord in a browser, open the target conversation, and open the panel. Identify or enter the scope, preview matching messages, then confirm irreversible deletion. Watch progress or press Stop to prevent further deletion requests.

Privacy & permissions

The script reads account and conversation identifiers and matching message metadata on Discord, and sends authenticated search and deletion requests to Discord. Credentials remain in page memory; language and delay are stored in userscript storage. The independent panel loads from xload.net and receives necessary scope identifiers, timestamp summaries, counts, and errors in memory, but not message bodies or credentials; chat content is not submitted to the xload server.
