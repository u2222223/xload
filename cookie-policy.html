Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They let the site remember your actions and preferences over time. Some cookies are set by us, and others by third parties whose services we use (such as Google).

Types of cookies we use

CategoryPurposeExamples
EssentialRequired for basic site functionality and to remember your consent choices.Consent preference
AnalyticsHelp us understand how visitors use the site, in aggregate and anonymously.Google Analytics
AdvertisingMake ads more relevant, measure performance and prevent fraud.Google AdSense / DART

Managing your preferences

When you first visit the site, our cookie banner lets you choose to "Accept all" or accept "Essential only". Your choice is stored on your device. You can also change your settings at any time by clearing our cookies in your browser.

In addition, you can control cookies at the browser level:

  • Block or delete cookies via your browser's privacy settings;
  • Opt out of personalised ads via Google Ads Settings;
  • Visit www.aboutcookies.org for general guidance.

Please note that blocking some cookies may affect the functionality of the site and how advertising is displayed.

Google Consent Mode

We use Google Consent Mode so Google services adapt their use of cookies and ads based on the consent you give. Until you choose "Accept all", advertising and analytics cookies remain disabled, in line with legal requirements in the EU/EEA and UK.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
